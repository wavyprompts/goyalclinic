Our Facility

Modern Infrastructure

Our clinic is designed to be a calming, stress-free environment. Equipped with ergonomic dental chairs, digital X-ray units (RVG), and intraoral cameras, we ensure accurate diagnosis and comfortable treatment.

Strict Sterilization

Patient safety is our #1 priority. We use Class B Autoclaves for 100% sterilization of all instruments. All needles, syringes, and cups are single-use disposables to prevent any cross-contamination.
